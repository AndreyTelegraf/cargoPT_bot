Moving costs in Porto

How much does a move cost in Porto?

The load, access, route, team and additional services determine the work required and should be described before an offer is prepared.

Published on 2026-07-24 · Reviewed by CargoPT Editorial Team

Direct answer

The cost depends on the specific service conditions

A move in Porto has no single price or useful average for every job. Cost depends on the inventory and volume, distance, floors, lift, stairs, loading area, number of movers and tasks such as packing, dismantling or assembly. To receive comparable estimates, give every carrier the same route, item list, photos and access conditions. Before choosing, confirm the vehicle, team, included tasks and how changes will be handled in writing.

Key points

Key points

  • List the main furniture, appliances and boxes.
  • Describe access at pickup and delivery.
  • Explain where the vehicle can stop.
  • Confirm the number of movers and included services.
  • Record the scope and conditions of the offer in writing.

How the service is estimated

The offer should reflect the vehicle, team, time and equipment required to carry out the move.

The more specific the description, the lower the risk of differences between the request and the actual service.

Volume

The number and size of the items help determine the vehicle.

Weight

Heavy items may require more movers or specific equipment.

Access

Stairs, lifts and corridors affect how the items can be moved.

Route

Pickup and delivery locations should be clearly identified.

Which access conditions should you describe?

State the floor, availability and size of the lift, approximate number of stairs and any obstacles.

Also explain the distance between the entrance and the possible parking location.

Services that may change the offer

Loading, unloading, dismantling, assembly, packing and materials should not be assumed to be included.

Confirm in writing which tasks are included and which remain your responsibility.

  • Number of movers.
  • Loading and unloading.
  • Dismantling and assembly.
  • Packing and materials.
  • Equipment for heavy items.

How to compare the prices received

A lower offer may include fewer tasks, fewer movers or different conditions.

Compare the complete service, availability, planned vehicle and how changes will be handled.

How to prepare the move

Finalise the inventory, prepare the boxes and identify fragile items before the agreed date.

Report any change in volume, access or timing in advance.

What real requests show about Porto

In an anonymised internal review of 77 requests submitted through the CargoPT form from 28 June to 2 September 2026, 15 involved at least one Porto address. The jobs were too varied and the sample too small for a responsible average price.

A useful estimate should therefore start from the exact route, inventory, volume, access and required tasks. These figures describe the reviewed sample; they do not predict a new move's price or guarantee offers.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know the total weight?

No. A detailed list of items, quantities and photographs is usually enough for an initial estimate.

Should I provide the size of the lift?

When possible, state whether the largest items fit in the lift and whether there are restrictions on its use.

How many movers are required?

It depends on the load, access and heavy items. The number should be confirmed in the offer.

Are packing materials included?

Not necessarily. Confirm whether boxes, blankets, film or other materials are included in the price.

What should I do if the inventory changes?

Report the change before the service and request confirmation of the new scope and price.

Is there a representative average price for a move in Porto?

There is no responsible average for every job: route, volume, access, team and services vary widely. Request an estimate for your exact inventory and conditions.
