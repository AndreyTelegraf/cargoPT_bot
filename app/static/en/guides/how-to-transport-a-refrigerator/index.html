Refrigerator transport

How to transport a refrigerator

Transport requires preparation, adequate protection, tilt control and a route compatible with the dimensions of the refrigerator.

Published on 2026-07-24 · Reviewed by CargoPT Editorial Team

Direct answer

Transport the refrigerator empty, protected and as vertical as possible

To transport a refrigerator, empty it, switch it off, defrost it and dry it completely. Remove or secure shelves, drawers, cables and other moving parts. Measure the appliance, doors, lifts, corridors and stairs before the job; protect its surfaces and plan the route to the vehicle. Keep the refrigerator as upright as possible while moving it and secure it so it cannot shift. If it was tilted significantly, check the model manual and follow the manufacturer's stated waiting time before switching it on again.

Key points

Main care

  • Prepare and completely dry equipment.
  • Measure the refrigerator and every point along the route; also send access photos.
  • Use helpers and means appropriate to the weight.
  • Keep the device as vertical as possible.
  • Attach it to the vehicle and follow the manual before starting it.

What to do before transport

The refrigerator must be empty, switched off, defrosted and dry. Shelves, drawers, cables and pipes need to be removed or immobilized.

Also check if there is water accumulated in the tray, dispenser or connections. Incomplete preparation may cause spills during movement.

Measure equipment and route

Record height, width and depth, including handles and other protruding elements. Measure doors, elevators, hallways, landings and stair turns.

Confirm where the vehicle can stop and the distance to the entrance. If the refrigerator does not fit at any point, it may be necessary to dismantle the property's doors or components authorized by the manufacturer.

  • External dimensions of the refrigerator.
  • Width of doors in both properties.
  • Space inside the elevator.
  • Curves and width of stairs.
  • Distance to the vehicle.
  • Obstacles and level differences.

How to move a refrigerator

A refrigerator is bulky, heavy and difficult to hold. It must not be dragged directly over the floor or moved only by doors or handles.

Use a suitable cart, straps and protection. The number of helpers must correspond to the weight, dimensions and access.

Protected floor

Use adequate protection to avoid risks and damage during exit and entry.

Transport cart

Choose equipment compatible with the weight and secure the refrigerator before moving it.

Straps

They must stabilize the device without crushing doors, panels or pipes.

Helpers

Stairs, gaps and large models may require a reinforced team.

In what position should it travel

The refrigerator must be transported as vertically as possible. The slope must be limited to what is necessary to overcome access points and use handling equipment.

When transport in a different position is unavoidable, consult the manual or manufacturer first. Allowed orientation and waiting time before connection vary between models.

How to protect the refrigerator in the vehicle

Place the equipment in a stable area, protected by blankets and without direct contact with hard or sharp items.

Attach it to suitable points on the vehicle to prevent movement, falls and impacts during braking and cornering. Do not place heavy items on the refrigerator.

What to do after delivery

Place the refrigerator in its final location, check that it is stable and confirm that cables and tubes have not been trapped or damaged.

Do not automatically turn it on right after arrival if it has been tilted. Consult the manufacturer's instructions and respect the period indicated for the model.

Frequently asked questions

Refrigerator Transport Questions

Can the refrigerator be transported lying down?

It is recommended to keep it as vertical as possible. If another position is unavoidable, confirm in advance in the manual or with the manufacturer which orientation is permitted.

Can I turn on the refrigerator immediately?

It depends on the position and model. If there was a tilt, follow the waiting period indicated by the manufacturer before turning on.

How many people are needed?

It depends on the weight, dimensions and access. Large models, stairs and lack of elevator may require more helpers.

Is it necessary to remove the refrigerator doors?

Only when access requires it and the procedure is permitted by the manufacturer. It must be done by someone with adequate knowledge.

What information should I include in the order?

Indicate dimensions, type of refrigerator, floors, elevators, stairs, parking, route and photographs of access.
