What you can move

For small and large moves

Apartments, houses, offices, furniture, appliances, pianos, building materials, or commercial and industrial loads — describe the route, cargo and access clearly so available carriers can assess the same job.

A request carriers can assess

Three blocks make the description useful

Route and timing

Give exact pickup and delivery locations plus the preferred date or time window.

Cargo, photos and tasks

List quantities, dimensions and heavy or fragile items. Add photos and say whether you need movers, packing, dismantling, assembly or special equipment.

Access

State floors, lifts, stairs, parking distance and obstacles at both addresses.

In an anonymised internal review of 77 requests submitted through the CargoPT form from 28 June to 2 September 2026, 102 pickup or delivery addresses were above ground level and 27 were marked as having no lift. The sample covers several transport types: it illustrates why access details matter, but does not predict price or guarantee offers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does transport cost?

The price depends on the distance, cargo volume, floors, elevator access, the chosen date and whether movers are needed. If carriers send offers, compare their prices and conditions before deciding.

Do I pay to submit a request?

No. Submitting a CargoPT request is free for anyone looking for transport. Carrier availability and offers are not guaranteed.

Can I move one item only?

Yes. You can request transport for furniture, appliances, a piano, boxes or other individual items. Specify what needs to be moved, the locations, access conditions and preferred date.

Can I move between cities?

Yes. You can request local transport or a move between cities in Portugal, including Lisbon, Porto, Cascais, Sintra, Oeiras and other locations.

What is CargoPT?

CargoPT is a request service for moves and transport. It organises the information you submit and may share it with suitable independent carriers; CargoPT does not perform the transport itself.

Are offers guaranteed?

No. The number of replies depends on carrier availability, route, date and job details. Submitting a complete request helps carriers assess it but does not guarantee an offer.
